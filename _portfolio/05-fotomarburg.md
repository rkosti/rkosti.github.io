*FAU Erlangen & Uni Marburg · 2019–2022*

Multimodal modeling on the 1.7M-image fotoMarburg corpus with art historians: bias audits of CLIP, DINO and VQGAN, generative pipelines, and explainable retrieval.

## What I did

- Multimodal modeling lead on the fotoMarburg corpus (1.7M images, DFG/SPP); applied generative AI and representation learning under severe data heterogeneity.
- Systematically probed CLIP, DINO, and VQGAN latent spaces; identified and published compositional, positional, and semantic biases in generative representations.
- Formulated semi-supervised, segmentation-guided GAN and diffusion synthesis pipelines for high-fidelity generation under data heterogeneity.
- Built automated cleaning, deduplication, and feature extraction pipelines across 1.7M heterogeneous assets for downstream multimodal training.
- Developed Image Composition Canvas extracting pose and spatial priors to model global action centers and semantic structures (Pattern Recognition 2023).
- Human pose estimation on ancient Greek vase paintings using style transfer and perceptual metrics.
- One-shot object detection with data-context augmentation on heterogeneous artwork datasets.
- Worked with digital art-history partners on the fotoMarburg corpus: aligned direction and goals in weekly syncs and operationalized their research questions into tangible ML goals.

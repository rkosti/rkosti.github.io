*Side project*

Generated background sound effects and video-aligned foreground audio for silent clips with the ElevenLabs API.

## What I did

- Personal project: used the ElevenLabs API to add audio to silent video clips: sound effects for the background of one clip, and video-aligned foreground audio for another by pairing voice generation with a video-to-text alignment model from a different provider.
